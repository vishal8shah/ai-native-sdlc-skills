## A useful community invitation

The ambition: 100 teams run one reproducible pilot each. Share the method, quality, costs and limitations—even when the pilot fails. Use the repository's pilot report template and synthetic or approved redacted data.

## Distribution principles

Lead with the runnable problem. Show actual output. Disclose limitations. Ask for a specific experiment or reproduction rather than generic engagement. Contribute to relevant discussions when you have something useful to add; avoid unsolicited bulk messages, artificial stars and inflated claims.
